What Can You Calculate?

Mean: The average of your numbers.

Median: The middle value when numbers are arranged in order.

Mode: The number that appears most frequently.

Range: The difference between the largest and smallest values.

Variance: A measurement of how spread out your numbers are.

Standard Deviation: Measures how far values typically vary from the mean.
